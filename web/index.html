Vektur Modular

This is the web test version. The synthesiser engine running here is the app's own C++ kernel, compiled to WebAssembly: the same oscillators, filters, envelopes, patching, modulation matrix, arpeggiator, sequencer, generative engine, 27 effects and MIDI engine as the iPad, iPhone, Mac and AUv3 builds.

Play with the on-screen keyboard, your computer keyboard (A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P) or a MIDI controller (browser permitting). Click an output jack and then an input jack to patch a cable; click a cable to remove it.
